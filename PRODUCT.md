# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Style-led shoppers in India who buy everyday jewellery for themselves: beaded pieces, semi-precious stones, and 316L stainless steel in gold and silver finishes. Confirmed by the user.

## Product Purpose

Papaya sells playful, handcrafted jewellery online. The storefront is a headless Next.js site on Shopify, which runs the catalog, cart, search, and checkout. Success means a shopper finds a piece they want and completes a prepaid order.

## Positioning

Beaded and semi-precious jewellery with an international, handcrafted feel, plus a stainless steel range in gold and silver finishes. The copy and layout treat jewellery as everyday self-expression, not occasion wear.

## Operating Context

- Shopping and payment run through Shopify: catalog, cart, search, and Shopify-hosted checkout.
- Prices are in INR. Shipping is within India only. Orders are prepaid only.
- Content is edited at /admin behind one shared password.
- The product catalog is placeholder until the real catalog arrives (`docs/seed/placeholder-products.csv`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed shopping rules: India-only shipping; prepaid orders only, with no Cash on Delivery; exchanges only, with no refunds for change of mind.
- Terms, privacy, and refund pages come from Shopify's shop policies, which checkout also reads.
- Information pages and diary posts are CMS content.
- Not connected yet: reviews, the contact inbox, customer accounts, and the newsletter (removed from the storefront).
- Dispatch is within 5–7 business days, per the FAQ.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Papaya. Logo: `public/brand/papaya-logo.png`.
- Voice, confirmed by the user: sunny and playful, as in "Endless Summer State of Mind" and "Crafted for golden hours." Tagline from the brand story: "Wear your mood. Own your style."

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand story, FAQ, and terms of service, supplied by the user. They now live in the CMS seed pages and Shopify's policies.
- Placeholder products and placeholder copy. Do not present them as real products, prices, or reviews.
- No customer reviews, sales figures, benchmarks, or real product photography exist yet. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Keep the jewellery the brightest thing on the page; layout and copy serve it.
2. State the rules before checkout: India-only shipping, prepaid payment, exchanges only.
3. Placeholder products, copy, and policies never read as real until they are replaced.
4. Make no claims the store cannot back: no invented prices, reviews, customers, or results.
