# Papaya: Brand Design Guide

> **Superseded.** The active design system is Citrus + Azure, see [design/stitch/tokens.md](design/stitch/tokens.md). This file is kept for reference only.

This document outlines the visual direction for Papaya. The focus is on a fresh, clean, and vibrant look, heavily utilizing a white and orange palette.

## The Core Vibe

Papaya is playful but clean. It feels fresh and inviting. The goal is to keep the layout light, simple, and easy to read. Think of clean lines, lots of empty space, and sudden pops of bright color to draw the eye.

## Color Palette

Leaning into white and orange is a strong choice, but it needs balance so it does not hurt the user's eyes. 

* **Primary Orange:** `#FF7A45` (A close match to the logo text). Use this for main buttons, important links, and small highlights. Do not use it for large blocks of background color.
* **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF`. This is your main background. It keeps things crisp and gives the orange room to stand out.
* **Soft Charcoal:** `#2D2D2D`. Pure black on pure white causes eye strain. Use this dark gray for all your main paragraph text and smaller headings. 
* **Light Peach (Accent):** `#FFF0EB`. A very pale, washed-out version of the primary orange. Use this for secondary button backgrounds, subtle highlights, or to separate different sections on a page without adding harsh lines.

## Typography

The logo uses a thick, playful serif font. We need to match that energy while keeping the main text easy to read.

* **Headings:** Choose a chunky serif font (like *Fraunces* or *Garamond*). Use this for big titles and section headers to match the logo's personality. 
* **Body Text:** Choose a clean, simple sans-serif font (like *Inter* or *DM Sans*). This ensures that long paragraphs are easy to read. Keep the font weight normal or light.

## Visuals & Imagery

* **Illustrations:** Keep the line-art style from the logo. Use thin, dark gray or black lines with occasional fills of the primary orange.
* **Photos:** If you use photos, they should have a warm tone. Lots of natural light, white backgrounds, and a bright, airy feel.
* **Spacing:** Give everything room to breathe. Do not cram text or images together. Extra white space makes the design look more expensive and professional.

## UI Elements (For Web & App)

* **Buttons:** Main buttons should be solid orange with white text. Make the corners slightly rounded (not completely sharp, but not full pills either) to match the friendly feel of the logo.
* **Cards:** If you put content in boxes, keep the box background white with a very soft, light gray shadow. 
* **Borders:** Avoid thick, dark borders. If you need to separate things, use a thin line in a very light gray or the light peach accent color.
